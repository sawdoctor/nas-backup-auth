Privacy policy

Sawdoctor NAS Backups is a personal backup utility operated by Sawdoctor for the owner's own Google Drive account.

Data accessed and purpose

The utility uses Google Drive file contents and metadata, including names, sizes, timestamps and checksums, to upload, list, verify, recover and retain the owner's backups. It requests Drive access through Google OAuth. Backup archives may contain private application and system data.

Storage and sharing

Backups are stored on owner-controlled storage and in the owner's Google Drive. OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored on owner-controlled systems so scheduled backups can run unattended. The operator does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or use it to train artificial intelligence models. Google processes data as the Drive storage provider. The operator does not share backup contents with other third parties except as required by law or explicitly directed by the owner.

Retention and control

Backup versions are retained according to the owner's configured retention settings. The owner can delete stored backups or revoke this utility's Google access from their Google Account permissions. Revoking access stops future authorised operations; it does not automatically delete existing local or Drive backups.

Google API use

This utility's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

This website

These static information pages contain no Google sign-in, forms, advertising or analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process routine connection logs under its own privacy policy.
